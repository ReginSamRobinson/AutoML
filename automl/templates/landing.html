Workflow

From spreadsheet to shortlist in three steps

No pipelines to write. Bring the data, say what you can spend, and compare what comes back.

  1. 01

    Bring a table

    CSV, Excel, JSON or Parquet. Pick the column to predict; classification or regression is detected for you.

    csvchurn_2024.csv → target: churned
  2. 02

    Set the budget

    Cap time per candidate and extra memory, set the score a model must reach, and choose the metric that ranks them.

    time ≤ 60 s mem ≤ 4 GB f1 ≥ 0.80
  3. 03

    Pick with evidence

    Every candidate is cross-validated and measured. See quality against cost, head to head, and keep the one that fits.

    #1 elastic net +0.4% · 12× faster

Capabilities

Built for the limits you actually have

Most AutoML tools chase the leaderboard. This one also answers whether the winner fits your hardware and your deadline.

Constraints are first-class

Time, memory and minimum score are checked for every candidate. Anything that breaks a limit is flagged with the reason, never silently ranked.

time0.4 / 60 s
memory18 / 4096 MB
accuracy0.979 ≥ 0.85

Measured, not estimated

Training time, peak memory above baseline and CPU are sampled while each candidate trains.

Copes with messy tables

  • row IDs dropped
  • dates → year · month · day
  • missing values imputed
  • latin-1 · semicolon CSVs

Classification or regression

Detected from the target column, with a one-click override when numeric labels look like values.

Interpretable-only mode

Restrict the search to linear models whose coefficients you can read and explain.

Stays on your machine

The dashboard is a local server. Datasets, settings and results never leave your computer.

Scoring

One score for quality and cost

Candidates are ranked on a single, published formula. When two models are close on accuracy, the cheaper one wins, and every term is visible in the leaderboard.

score = 0.7 · quality − 0.3 · cost − 0.1 · violations
Quality 70%
Cost 30%
time 50% memory 30% cpu 20%

Quality and each cost are scaled 0–1 within the run. Each broken limit costs another 0.1.
